# Content usage analysis

For this assignment, I analyzed JetBrains article about discontinuation of JetBrains IDE Fleet: https://blog.jetbrains.com/fleet/2025/12/the-future-of-fleet/

Spreadsheet with my findings: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1js68QZD8jFLb2auvIfnbzE5MzGzg_Uq9FSSA966AQgY/edit?usp=sharing

This article announces that support of Fleet will be discontinued and it will be no longer available for download from December 22, 2025. It was developed as an experiment in building a new generation of JetBrains IDEs, but maintaining two general-purpose IDE families created confusion and diluted focus. JetBrains is redirecting the Fleet team and technology toward a new product focused on agentic development.

I chose specifically this article, since Fleet started as a very ambitious project that was much discussed among developers, and the discontinuation new of Fleet was one of the most important new made by JetBrains in recent months. This article should be referenced, discussed and reused outside its original context.
